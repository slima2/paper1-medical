\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Medical tasks mapping a low-cost source modality onto a costly target
are reported as a cohort-average metric that hides which targets are
reachable.
We treat them as imperfect translation and report per-target
translatability.
Our three-regime taxonomy---encoder saturation, inter-pattern
co-occurrence, out-of-ontology---is the redundant, synergistic and
unique atoms of a partial information decomposition of the source
ontology against the encoder, paired with a Conclusive/Inconclusive
verdict and the six-level \lchai{} explanation stack.
On TCGA-LUAD (687 slides, 668 patients) TP53 and EGFR saturate the
encoder, KRAS is synergistic, STK11 and KEAP1 fall outside it; KRAS is
Inconclusive at 0.609 yet 0.965 on one slide.
A cohort transfer to CPTAC-LUAD fails---TP53 collapses from 0.72 to
0.53 off-the-shelf and 0.48 after stain normalisation---so no
assignment is near-intrinsic.
The framework transfers: on {PTB-XL} it recovers physiological state
from the electrocardiogram (AUROC 0.90), and supports a pre-registered
glioma test under {VASARI}.
\end{abstract}
