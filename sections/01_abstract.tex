\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Medical tasks predicting a target the source modality cannot
directly measure are reported as a cohort-average hiding which
targets are reachable.
We treat them as imperfect translation, reporting per-target
translatability.
Our three-regime taxonomy---encoder saturation, inter-pattern
co-occurrence, out-of-ontology---corresponds to the redundant,
synergistic and unique atoms of a partial information decomposition of
the source ontology against the encoder, paired with a
Conclusive/Inconclusive verdict and a six-level explanation stack.
On TCGA-LUAD TP53 and EGFR saturate the
encoder, KRAS is synergistic, STK11 and KEAP1 fall outside it; KRAS is
Inconclusive at 0.609 yet 0.965 on one slide.
Frozen transfer to CPTAC-LUAD collapses (TP53 0.72 to 0.53), yet
re-fitting there recovers 0.70: the transported model fails, not the
coupling.
The framework transfers: on the public electrocardiogram dataset
{PTB-XL} it recovers physiological state (AUROC 0.90), and a glioma MRI
instantiation reproduces the regimes---IDH saturated, 1p/19q
fusion-recovered, and MGMT Inconclusive at full power.
\end{abstract}
