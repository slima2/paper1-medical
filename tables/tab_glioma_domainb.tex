\begin{flushleft}\footnotesize
Mean test $\auroc\pm$SD over five patient-disjoint folds of the TCGA-LGG
cohort carrying co-registered preoperative MRI, real VASARI scores and a
molecular call (up to $n=63$ subjects; IDH resolved on four folds, one
test fold being single-class). Source channel $\mathbf{P}$: the $\sim$26
neuroradiologist-scored VASARI features (min--max rescaled); encoder
channel $\mathbf{E}$: a 2.5D CNN with \abmil{} over axial slices of the
four co-registered sequences. Conditions match the LUAD protocol:
$\mathrm{B2}$ encoder only, $\mathrm{B3}$ VASARI only, $\mathrm{PI}$ concat
fusion, $\mathrm{FC}$ encoder $+$ 2-additive Choquet over the VASARI
features. The regime is read from the decomposition, not the top estimate.
\textbf{IDH} is encoder-saturated (regime~i): $\mathrm{B2}$ alone already
reaches $0.856$ and the fusion arms add nothing beyond a fold's SD.
\textbf{1p/19q} is a complementarity case (regime~ii): neither channel
alone is informative ($\mathrm{B2}\!=\!0.60$, $\mathrm{B3}\!=\!0.50$) yet
their fusion recovers the target ($\mathrm{PI}\!=\!0.74$, $+0.14$ over the
encoder). The \emph{non-additive} form of that complementarity---the
pre-registered $\mathrm{FC}\!>\!\mathrm{PI}$ signature seen for KRAS in
LUAD---is \emph{not} resolved at this sample size: the learned Choquet
pair interactions shrink to $\lvert I_{jk}\rvert\!\lesssim\!0.005$ and
$\mathrm{FC}$ trails $\mathrm{PI}$, so the interaction read is deferred to
the full-power v2 cohort (adding BraTS-TCGA-GBM and the test arm). The
\textbf{sex} control confirms the guard against spurious certification:
VASARI carries no sex signal ($\mathrm{B3}\!=\!0.30$, below chance) and the
verdict is correctly Inconclusive. MGMT is unavailable in this clinical
source and is reported underpowered/Inconclusive (v2 item).
\end{flushleft}
