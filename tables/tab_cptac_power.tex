% Table tab:cptac_power — observed positive-set sizes for the six target
% genes in the CPTAC-LUAD cohort (n=110), used to make the within-domain
% cohort-transfer pre-registration explicit about which targets it can and
% cannot estimate. Prevalences are the actual non-silent, patient-level
% mutation frequencies queried from cBioPortal study luad_cptac_2020
% (Gillette et al., Cell 2020), reproducible via scripts/fetch_cptac_labels.py.
% n_+ is the observed positive count at n=110; the estimation floor is the
% n_+=37 at which RBM10 is only underpowered in the larger TCGA-LUAD cohort.
\footnotesize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{6pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
\begin{tabular}{l c c c}
\toprule
\textbf{Gene} & \textbf{Regime (LUAD)} &
  \textbf{CPTAC prevalence} & \textbf{$n_+$ at $n\!=\!110$ (floor $37$)} \\
\midrule
TP53  & (i) saturation & $53.6\%$ & $59$ \quad above \\
EGFR  & (i) saturation & $34.5\%$ & $38$ \quad above \\
KRAS  & (ii) synergy   & $30.0\%$ & $33$ \quad below (margin) \\
STK11 & (iii)          & $18.2\%$ & $20$ \quad below \\
KEAP1 & (iii)          & $10.9\%$ & $12$ \quad below \\
RBM10 & estimation floor & $4.5\%$ & $5$ \quad below \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\begin{flushleft}\footnotesize
Prevalences are the observed non-silent, patient-level mutation
frequencies queried from cBioPortal study \texttt{luad\_cptac\_2020}
($n\!=\!110$)\cite{gillette2020cptacluad,cptacluad_data} and are
reproducible with \texttt{scripts/fetch\_cptac\_labels.py}; $n_+$ is the
observed positive count and the floor is the $n_+\!=\!37$ at which
\emph{RBM10} is only underpowered in the $n\!=\!687$ TCGA-LUAD cohort
reported here. At the full $n\!=\!110$ two genes clear the floor
(\emph{TP53}, \emph{EGFR}); in the genotyped-with-imaging subset actually
scored ($n\!=\!106$, \cref{tab:cptac_transfer}) only \emph{TP53} clears it
($n_+\!=\!55$) while \emph{EGFR} falls just short ($n_+\!=\!36$). \emph{KRAS}
falls below the floor and the regime-(iii) genes and \emph{RBM10} fall well
below, so the transfer is, by construction, well-powered only for the single
saturated target \emph{TP53} and underpowered for exactly the untranslatable
candidates it would most need to check.
\end{flushleft}
